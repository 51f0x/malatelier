## Brand Colors

### Primary Color: Malatelier Red
- **Hex**: `#e84747`
- **RGB**: rgb(232, 71, 71)
- **Usage**: Primary brand color, buttons, links, accents
- **Source**: Extracted from official website CSS (`--custom-title-color`)

### Secondary & Accent Colors
- **Primary Light**: `#FF6B6B` - Lighter shade for hover states
- **Primary Dark**: `#D63031` - Darker shade for pressed states
- **Secondary**: `#F39C12` - Warm golden orange for complementary accents
- **Accent**: `#E74C3C` - Bright coral red for highlights

## Typography

While not directly sourced from the Malatelier Brig website, the theme uses Material Design typography principles:
- **Headings**: Roboto (sans-serif)
- **Body**: Lora (serif) for comfortable reading
- **Code**: Roboto Mono (monospace)

## About Malatelier Brig

Malatelier Brig is an art studio located in Brig, Switzerland at:
**Alte Simplonstrasse 35, 3900 Brig**

The studio has been operating since 1984 and offers:
- Children's painting courses (Kindermalen)
- Modeling and sculpting
- Pottery (Töpfern)
- Adult art workshops
- Creative courses for all ages
